Step 5: Stream Your Match 📺

Every match must be streamed live on YouTube. Here's why:

  • It stops cheating. If someone cheats, we'll see it on the stream. No stream means we can't check — and that's not fair on anyone.
  • It protects you. If someone says you cheated, your stream proves you didn't.
  • Your match lives on YouTube forever — friends, family, and future Couch Cup players can watch it anytime. 🎮

First time only — set up your YouTube:
You need a free YouTube account. If you don't have one, go to youtube.com and create one. It takes 2 minutes and it's completely free.
Then link it to your PS5:

  1. Go to Settings on your PS5
  2. Select Users and Accounts
  3. Select Linked Services
  4. Select YouTube and follow the steps

Before every match — turn on recording:
This is important. YouTube doesn't save your stream automatically. You need to switch this on or your match won't be saved.

  1. Go to studio.youtube.com on your phone or computer
  2. Click Go Live
  3. Click Stream Settings
  4. Turn on Save replay

Do this before every match. If your stream isn't saved we can't verify anything if there's a dispute.

Before every match — go live:

  1. Open EA FC 26 on your PS5
  2. Press the Create button on your controller
  3. Select Broadcast
  4. Choose YouTube
  5. Set the stream title to: Couch Cup — [Your PSN] vs [Opponent PSN]
  6. Hit Go Live before the match kicks off
  7. Copy your stream link and include it when you submit your score

Important:

  • Start your stream before the match begins
  • Keep it live until the final whistle
  • Don't end the stream early — we need the full match recorded

Stuck? Email us at hello@couchcup.co.za and we'll help you out.

Step 6: Submit Your Score
