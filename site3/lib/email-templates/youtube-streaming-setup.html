Streaming your matches live on YouTube is one of the tournament conditions, as set out on the conditions page. To go live from your own account, you first need to request streaming access. YouTube can take up to 24 hours to switch it on, so if you leave it to match day you could miss your window and forfeit or delay your match.

It takes two minutes, and it's easiest on a PC or laptop. You can do it from a mobile browser too — it'll just be a bit small.

  1. Go to https://studio.youtube.com/
  2. Click "Create" (top right)
  3. Click "Go Live" from the menu
  4. Follow the steps from there

Do this at least 24 hours before your kick-off time. Don't leave it late.
